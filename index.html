FAQ

How do I play?

Stars fall in groups of three. Move them left and right, tap to change their order, and drop them. Three or more stars of the same color in a row (across, down or diagonally) disappear, and the stars above fall and can set off chains. Clear the stars a stage asks for to light up that star.

Why are the stars different colors?

Each stage's color is the real color of that star, which comes from its surface temperature: blue stars are the hottest, then white, yellow, orange, and red stars are the coolest.

How do I get items?

Row Comet, Column Comet and Black Hole are earned by playing: every stage you win, a bonus for a 1st-magnitude win, a set for every finished constellation, and one every 5 levels in Classic. When you run out you can watch an optional video for one (up to 5 a day).

What is in Starfall Pro?

A one-time purchase that removes the banner ad, lets you continue and preview the next pieces without videos, and opens the Southern Sky pack (Southern Cross, Centaurus, Carina, Southern Triangle) and the Eastern Sky pack (Northern Dipper and the Four Symbols of the 28 mansions).

I bought Pro on a new phone. How do I get it back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the menu and tap Restore purchase.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Your stars and progress are saved only on your device, so deleting the app removes them.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Where do the star facts come from?

Star positions, brightness and colors come from the Yale Bright Star Catalog; star names follow the IAU list; distances and facts were checked against current sources. If something looks wrong, please tell us.
